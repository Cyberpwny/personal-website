How this website handles data

This is a static portfolio website. It does not use analytics, advertising technology, tracking pixels, embedded social-media feeds, contact forms, or non-essential cookies.

To deliver and protect the website, Cloudflare may process technical request data such as IP address, time of access, requested file, referrer, browser, operating system, and security-related request information. Cloudflare operates a global network and may process data outside the European Economic Area. Cloudflare states that transfers are covered by the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where applicable and otherwise by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and additional safeguards. Further information is available in Cloudflare's privacy policy and Data Processing Addendum.

No Cloudflare Web Analytics, operator-side visitor analytics, or advertising services are enabled for this website.
